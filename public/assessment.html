C. Speaking check teacher only · 5 minutes

Ask the questions aloud. Part 2 is for IELTS students (or strong Spoken English students).

Part 1 — Introduction

  1. What is your full name, and where are you from?
  2. Do you work or are you a student? Tell me about it.
  3. What do you usually do in your free time?
  4. Why do you want to improve your English?
  1. Tell me a little about your family.
  2. Where do you live? What do you like about that place?
  3. What kind of food do you enjoy most?
  4. What would you like to be doing five years from now?

Part 2 — Short talk (1 minute)

Describe a person who has helped you in your life. You should say: who this person is · how you know them · how they helped you — and explain why you are thankful to them.
Describe a place you would like to visit. You should say: where it is · how you know about it · what you would do there — and explain why you want to go there.
