Spoken English

Confidence-first speaking practice for daily life, interviews, and work.

Book a Free Demo ClassSee PricingApply for Admission

This course is built for students who understand English but freeze when they need to speak it. We focus on breaking the habit of translating from Punjabi/Hindi in your head, building real conversational fluency through daily speaking practice, grammar in context, and vocabulary you'll actually use — not just textbook lists.

What's Covered

The curriculum, module by module.

Grammar in Context

Simple Present through Conditionals, taught through speaking practice rather than isolated drills.

Vocabulary & Idioms

250+ everyday words and common idioms, drilled with visual presentations and games.

Pronunciation

Word and sentence stress, plus common sounds that trip up Punjabi speakers specifically.

Real Conversation

Interviews, phone calls, workplace English, and everyday situations — practiced live, not scripted.
