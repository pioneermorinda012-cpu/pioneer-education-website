## WhatsApp Opening Sequence

This is what fires the moment someone taps "Send WhatsApp Message" from the ad. Meta pre-fills their first message from the ad's default text — reply within a few minutes if you can; response speed is the biggest lever on whether a click becomes a booked demo.

**Auto-reply / first human reply (send within minutes):**
> Hi! 👋 Thanks for reaching out to Pioneer Education, Morinda.
> Quick question so I can point you right — are you looking at IELTS Academic (university) or General Training (PR/visa)?
> Either way, your first class is completely free, no payment or commitment. Want me to check available demo slots this week?

**If they go quiet after 1 message (follow up ~2 hours later):**
> Just checking in — no pressure at all! If it's easier, I can share our batch timings (morning 9–1, evening also available) and you can pick whatever works. 😊

**If they ask about pricing:**
> Group Class is ₹2,499/month (live classes 5 days/week, max 8 students, weekly feedback + certificate). We also have 1-to-1 coaching at ₹4,999/month if you want fully personal sessions. But honestly — come try the free demo first and see which fits before deciding anything.
