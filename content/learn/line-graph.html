4Grammar: choosing the tense

Past

Past simple — events one after another in the past: There was a significant rise in 1964. Then, the figure dipped sharply in 1980.
Past perfect — what had happened by a past time: The figure dipped sharply and had reached 5 by 1980.

Present

Present simple — regular events, or a value now: There is a significant rise at 6 am every morning. · The number of cases now stands at 158.
Present perfect — from the past up to now: There has been a significant rise since 2013, and the figure now stands at 15,000.

Future — for predictions

Nobody is certain about the future, so show a little uncertainty.

PatternExample
will probably / likely + verbThe figure will probably reach 15,000 in 2050.
should + verbThe figure should reach 15,000 in 2050.
is likely to + verbThe figure is likely to reach 15,000 in 2050.
It seems likely that ... willIt seems likely that the figure will reach 15,000 in 2050.
Predictions / Forecasts / Estimates + show / indicate thatForecasts indicate that the figure will reach 800 in 2050.
It is predicted / expected / estimated thatIt is expected that the figure will reach 800 in 2050.
The figure is predicted / expected + toThe figure is expected to reach 800 in 2050.

The same paragraph, past → future

In 1999, the proportion of people using the Internet in the USA was about 20%. The figures for Canada and Mexico were lower, at about 10% and 5% respectively. In 2005, Internet usage in both the USA and Canada rose to around 70% of the population, while the figure for Mexico reached just over 25%.
In 2020, the proportion of people using the Internet in the USA is expected to be about 20%. The figures for Canada and Mexico are likely to be lower, at about 10% and 5% respectively. In 2050, it is predicted that Internet usage in both the USA and Canada will rise to around 70% of the population, while the figure for Mexico should reach just over 25%.

7Task: Dubai gold sales

Writing Task 1 · about 20 minutes · at least 150 words

The graph below gives information about Dubai gold sales in 2002.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Dubai gold sales, 2002
Plan first: highest point (March), lowest points (July and September), and the overall picture (December almost back to the January level).
Show the sample answer
Sample answer

The diagram illustrates how gold sales in Dubai changed over a period of 12 months.

Overall, gold sales in Dubai remained relatively unchanged at the end of 2002 despite violent fluctuations throughout the year. Besides, they were at their highest in March, while the weakest sales figures could be observed in July and September.

In the first month of 2002, they stood at 200 million dirhams and rose slightly to reach about 225 million in February. This was followed by another increase, although much steeper, in March, when sales were almost 125 million dirhams higher than in February. However, this upward trend was suddenly broken and sales plummeted dramatically over the next 4 months to reach a little over 100 million dirhams in July. August sales saw a significant rise back to January levels as figures nearly doubled, but they dropped again in September to the same level as they were in July. There was a small increase of about 60 million dirhams in October, after which the figure levelled off and remained relatively static over the last two months of 2002.

(182 words)
Corrected from the slide: October sales rose from about 120 to 180 million, an increase of about 60 million, not 100.

8Task: fish and meat consumption

Writing Task 1 · about 20 minutes · at least 150 words

The graph below shows the consumption of fish and some different kinds of meat in a European country between 1979 and 2004.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Consumption of fish and meat, grams per person per week
Show the sample answer
Sample answer

The diagram shows how much of different kinds of meat and fish was consumed in a country in Europe from 1979 to 2004. Overall, although beef was the most popular type of meat at first, it was replaced by chicken towards the end of the period. In contrast, fish remained the least significant throughout the period.

In the first year, beef was the most popular, with a consumption of about 225 grams per person per week. Then, this figure experienced a sudden plunge of almost 50 before surging to about 230 in 1984. Following this, there were some fluctuations until 1989, after which it decreased dramatically to around half its original level in 2004.

The figure for lamb was initially almost as high as that for chicken (around 150 grams per person per week). However, while the former declined gradually and significantly to approximately 60 in the last year, the latter saw considerable growth, overtook beef in 1989 and peaked at about 250 in 2004.

Fish consumption was originally approximately 60 grams and experienced a small fall of about 20 over the period.

(183 words)
Corrected from the slide: fish fell from about 60 to under 40 grams, a fall of about 20, not 10. Also tidied: “around half as high as its original level” → “around half its original level”, and “outstripped beef consumption in 1989, peaking at 250” → “overtook beef in 1989 and peaked at about 250”.

9Quick check

Sales went from 200 to 350 in one month. Which is best?
A jump of 150 in one month is a sharp (fast, large) rise.
Choose the correct preposition: “Sales peaked ___ 350 million ___ March.”
at + value, in + month or year.
Which word has no adverb form for trends?
Write “a huge increase”, not “increased hugely”.
“There has been a rise since 2013.” Which tense is this?
since + a past time up to now → present perfect.
Which sentence describes the future with suitable caution?
Predictions are not certain: is expected to, is likely to, should.
Which phrase is used for the last event?
Finally / Ultimately / … before finally …
