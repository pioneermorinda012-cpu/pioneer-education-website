Speaking Part 2 — Places: vocabulary, cue cards and model lines

Every cue card below carries the vocabulary from your lessons that actually fits it, printed above the card so the words are revised before the answer is planned. Under each bullet there are two model lines: a basic one that is correct and safe, and an advanced one built on the collocations from Lessons 6, 20, 21, 38, 49 and 50.

Say the basic line until it comes without thinking, then move up. Change the details — a memorised answer is marked down, an adapted one is not.

Describe a special hotel you stayed in

Use these words — from Lesson 50 · Everyday Verbs, Lesson 6 · Houses & Apartments
  • overlooking the ocean — with the ocean visible from it
  • a view of the city skyline — you can see the shape of the city from there
  • recently renovated — repaired and improved not long ago
  • fully furnished — already has all the furniture in it
  • a spacious living room — a large, roomy living room
  • rather cramped — small and uncomfortable
  • a short-term rental — a place rented for a short period
  • have a blast — enjoy yourself enormously
  • take advantage of — make good use of an opportunity
You should say:
  • Where it is
  • What it is like
  • Why you went there
  • And how you felt about it
Where it is
BasicThe hotel is in ___, near the beach.
AdvancedIt's in ___, right on the seafront — the sort of place where the sea is the first thing you hear in the morning.
What it is like
BasicIt is a big hotel with clean rooms and a swimming pool.
AdvancedIt had clearly been recently renovated: the rooms were fully furnished in a simple way, and ours had a balcony overlooking the ocean.
Why you went there
BasicI went there with my family for a holiday.
AdvancedWe went for a short family break — my father had been working long hours and we all badly needed a change of scenery.
And how you felt about it
BasicI felt very happy there and I enjoyed my stay.
AdvancedI had a blast, honestly. I took full advantage of the pool, and I came home feeling like I'd been away for a month rather than four days.
Finishing line
BasicSo that is the special hotel I stayed in.
AdvancedIt's the one hotel I'd go back to without checking anywhere else first.

  • When you visited
  • With whom you visited
  • What you liked there
Name and location of the place
BasicI visited ___, which is in my city.
AdvancedI'd like to talk about ___, which sits a little way out on the outskirts of the city.
When you visited
BasicI visited it last year during the festival.
AdvancedI went during the festival last year, which is the busiest time — not the quietest, but certainly the most alive.

Speak about a peaceful place you visit regularly

Use these words — from Glossary page, Lesson 20 · City, Lesson 21 · Countryside, Lesson 50 · Everyday Verbs
  • peace and quiet — calm, with no noise or disturbance
  • a change of scenery — a change of surroundings
  • take a break — pause from what you are doing

  • a winding footpath — a walking path with many turns
  • in my element — doing exactly what I enjoy
  • noise pollution — unwanted, excessive noise
You should say:
  • Name and location of the place
  • When and how you go there
  • Describe the place
  • What you do there
Name and location of the place
BasicThe place I go to is a park near my house.
AdvancedIt's a stretch of open ground on the banks of the canal, about fifteen minutes' walk from my house.
When and how you go there
BasicI usually go there in the evening, on foot.
AdvancedI walk there most evenings, usually when I need to take a break from studying rather than at any fixed time.
Describe the place
BasicIt is a quiet place with trees and a small path.
AdvancedThere's a winding footpath along the water, lined with old trees, and after the noise pollution of the main road it feels like another town entirely.
What you do there
BasicI walk there and sometimes I sit and listen to music.
AdvancedMostly I walk and let my head empty. I'm rather in my element there, which is strange to admit about a patch of grass.

BasicThat is the peaceful place I visit regularly.
AdvancedHalf an hour there does more for me than an evening in front of a screen.

  • What this company does
  • Where this company is
  • And explain why you would like to work in this company
The name of this company
BasicThe company I would like to work in is ___.
AdvancedThe company I'd most like to join is ___, which most people in my field would recognise.
What this company does
BasicIt is a company that makes software.
AdvancedIt builds software for other businesses — unglamorous work, but the scale of it is what interests me.

AdvancedIf I could take advantage of one opportunity in the next five years, it would be that one.

Describe a short journey that you disliked

Use these words — from Lesson 20 · City, Lesson 38 · Starting & Finishing, Lesson 49 · Confusing Words, Lesson 50 · Everyday Verbs, Lesson 6 · Houses & Apartments
  • traffic jams — lines of stopped traffic
  • a disastrous start — a very bad beginning
  • the hectic pace — the fast, chaotic rhythm of life
  • strewn with litter — covered untidily with rubbish
  • rather cramped — small and uncomfortable
  • come to an end — finish
  • noise pollution — unwanted, excessive noise
  • keep an eye on — watch or monitor something
You should say:
  • Where you went
  • Who you went with
  • What you did
  • And explain why you disliked this journey
Where you went
BasicI went from my city to another city by bus.
AdvancedIt was a bus journey of about four hours between two cities — on paper, nothing at all.
Who you went with
BasicI went with my cousin.
AdvancedI was travelling with my cousin, which at least gave me someone to complain to.
What you did
BasicWe sat in the bus and waited. The journey was very slow.
AdvancedVery little. We sat in traffic jams for most of it, took turns keeping an eye on the luggage, and watched the time disappear.
And explain why you disliked this journey
BasicI disliked it because the bus was crowded and it was too hot.
AdvancedIt got off to a disastrous start — the bus was ninety minutes late — and it never recovered. The seats were rather cramped, the floor was strewn with litter, and four hours became seven.
Finishing line
BasicSo that is the journey I disliked.
AdvancedWhen it finally came to an end I was so relieved I forgot to be angry.

You should say:
  • What place it was
  • Why you went there
  • How it was affected by pollution
  • And explain how you felt about this situation
What place it was
BasicIt was a river in my city which is very dirty now.
AdvancedIt was a river that runs through the city — one that older people in my family remember swimming in.
Why you went there
BasicI went there with my college for a project.
AdvancedWe went as part of a college project on water quality, so I was looking at it more carefully than I ever had before.
How it was affected by pollution
BasicThe water was black and there was a lot of rubbish.

AdvancedSince that project I've paid far more attention to where our own rubbish ends up.

Describe an interesting public place in your hometown that you like to go to

Use these words — from Lesson 20 · City, Lesson 50 · Everyday Verbs
  • an open-air market — a market held outdoors
  • street vendors — people selling things in the street
  • bustling streets — streets busy and full of people
  • quaint shops — charming, old-fashioned shops
  • the local cuisine — the typical food of that region
  • trendy cafés — modern, stylish, popular cafés
  • break the ice — start conversation in an awkward group
  • have a blast — enjoy yourself enormously
  • cobblestone streets — old streets paved with round stones
You should say:
  • Where it is
  • How often you go there
  • What you do there
  • And explain why you think it is interesting
Where it is
BasicIt is the main market in my town.
AdvancedIt's the old market square at the centre of town, which has been in the same place for well over a century.
How often you go there
BasicI go there once or twice a week.
AdvancedOnce or twice a week, and almost always on Sunday, when the open-air market takes over the whole square.
What you do there
BasicI buy things there and sometimes I eat street food.
AdvancedI buy whatever we need from the street vendors, eat something I shouldn't, and usually end up in one of the trendy cafés that have appeared along the edge.
And explain why you think it is interesting
BasicIt is interesting because many different people come there.
AdvancedBecause everybody is there — the quaint shops that have been run by the same families for decades, right beside new cafés full of students. It's also the easiest place in town to break the ice with a stranger.
Finishing line
BasicThat is the public place I like to go to.
AdvancedIf someone visited my town for one afternoon, that is where I would take them.
