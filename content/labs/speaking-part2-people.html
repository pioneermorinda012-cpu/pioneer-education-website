Speaking Part 2 — People: vocabulary, cue cards and model lines

Each cue card below carries the vocabulary from your lessons that actually fits it, printed above the card so you revise the words before you plan the answer. Under every bullet there are two model lines: a basic one that is correct and safe, and an advanced one using the collocations from the lessons.

Use the basic line until it is automatic, then move to the advanced one. Do not memorise either — change the details so the answer is yours.

Cue card 1

Describe a character or personality of yours

Use these words — from Lesson 4 · Character & Behaviour
  • an outgoing personality — friendly and sociable by nature
  • painfully shy — extremely shy
  • come out of your shell — become less shy
  • speak your mind — say exactly what you think
  • have a thick skin — not be easily hurt by criticism
  • brutally honest — honest even when it hurts
  • fiercely loyal — loyal without any hesitation
  • swallow your pride — stop being proud and admit you were wrong
  • a mean streak — a small cruel part of someone's character
You should say:
  • What it is
  • How it affects your life
  • Where you get it from
  • And how you feel about it
What it is
BasicI'd like to talk about one part of my character, which is that I am very honest.
AdvancedThe trait I'd single out is honesty — I've always been the sort of person who speaks their mind, even when it would be easier not to.
How it affects your life
BasicIt helps me because people trust me and tell me their problems.
AdvancedIt cuts both ways, really. It has won me the respect of my friends, but being brutally honest has also cost me a few of them.
Where you get it from
BasicI think I got it from my father. He is also a very honest man.
AdvancedI'd put it down to my father. He was raised by strict parents and he passed that same directness straight on to me.
And how you feel about it
BasicI feel good about it, but sometimes I think I should be more careful.
AdvancedOn balance I'm glad of it, though I've had to learn the hard way that honesty without kindness is just rudeness.
Finishing line
BasicSo that's the part of my personality I'd choose.
AdvancedSo that's the trait I'd point to — not always comfortable to live with, but the one I'd least like to lose.

Describe a creative inventor or musician (or a creative person you admire)

Use these words — from Lesson 38 · Starting & Finishing, Lesson 39 · Success & Failure, Lesson 4 · Character & Behaviour
  • a vivid imagination — a strong, colourful imagination
  • impressive accomplishments — achievements worth admiring
  • a phenomenal success — an extraordinary success
  • make a breakthrough — suddenly advance past an obstacle
  • the crowning achievement — the greatest achievement of a career
  • humble beginnings — a poor or modest start in life
  • pull it off — succeed at something difficult
  • win the respect of — earn people's respect
You should say:
  • Who this person is
  • What this person does / what area of creativity they work in
  • How you know about this person
  • Why you think this person is creative / what work you particularly admire
Who this person is
BasicI want to talk about a musician called ___. He is very famous in my country.
AdvancedThe person I'd choose is ___, a musician whose work I've followed since I was at school.
What this person does / what area of creativity they work in
BasicHe writes songs and plays the guitar.
AdvancedHe works right across the creative process — writing, composing and producing — which is unusual for one person.
How you know about this person
BasicI first heard about him on the internet and then I started listening to his songs.
AdvancedI came across his work almost by accident online, and I've since lost count of how many hours I've spent listening to it.
Why you think this person is creative / what work you particularly admire
BasicHe is creative because his songs are different from other singers' songs.
AdvancedWhat marks him out is that he has a genuinely vivid imagination — he made a breakthrough with an album nobody expected, and pulling that off twice is what won him the respect of other musicians.
Finishing line
BasicThat is the creative person I admire most.
AdvancedThat album, for me, remains the crowning achievement of his career so far.

Describe a family (not your own family) that you like

Use these words — from Lesson 1 · Family, Lesson 2 · Relationships, Lesson 39 · Success & Failure, Lesson 5 · Feelings

  • extended family — uncles, cousins, grandparents and so on

  • a carefree childhood — a childhood with no worries
  • blissfully happy — perfectly happy
  • bring out the best in people — make others perform at their best
  • make friends — form friendships
You should say:
  • Where this family lives
  • Who the members of the family are
  • How you know them
  • What each person in this family does in life
  • And explain why you like this family
Where this family lives
BasicThis family lives near my house, in the same street.
AdvancedThey live just a few doors down from us, which is how we got to know them in the first place.
Who the members of the family are
BasicThere are four people: the mother, the father and two children.
AdvancedThere are four of them — the parents and two children — although their extended family seems to be in and out of the house constantly.
How you know them
BasicI know them because my friend is their daughter.
AdvancedI made friends with the daughter at college, and after a few visits I more or less became part of the furniture.
What each person in this family does in life
BasicThe father is a teacher, the mother works in a bank, and the children are students.
AdvancedThe father teaches, the mother works in a bank, and both children are still studying — so the house runs on a fairly tight schedule.
And explain why you like this family
BasicI like them because they are kind and they are always happy together.
AdvancedWhat I like is the atmosphere: they're not wealthy, but they're a genuinely close-knit family, and they bring out the best in each other.
Finishing line
BasicThat is the family I like most.
AdvancedThey're the family I'd point to if someone asked me what a happy home looks like.
