Big feelings, and the right word for each

Fifty-four words for five families of emotion. The useful part is not the list — it is the strength. “Peeved”, “annoyed” and “infuriated” are all anger, and using the wrong one makes an answer sound strange rather than advanced.

Pick a family, read the ladder from mild to very strong, then tap any word to see what it means and how to use it. The quiz at the bottom asks you to tell close words apart, which is the only test that matters.
